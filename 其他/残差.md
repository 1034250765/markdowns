## residual connection



Good morning/afternoon everyone,

Thank you for your attention. It is our great pleasure to present our poster on the groundbreaking work from Microsoft Research: **"Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition."**

In the field of deep learning, the depth of a network is crucial for its performance. However, as many of you may know, the deeper the network, the harder it is to train. While traditional views often attribute this difficulty to vanishing gradients or overfitting, this research uncovers a more fundamental challenge: the **Degradation Problem**.

This problem manifests as a phenomenon where, after a certain depth, accuracy not only stops improving but significantly deteriorates—and this happens on the *training set*. This indicates it is fundamentally an **optimization problem**, not an issue of model capacity or overfitting.

So, how can we break through this depth barrier? The core of our poster presents a revolutionary solution: the **Deep Residual Learning Framework**.

The core idea is ingeniously simple. Instead of having stacked nonlinear layers directly fit a complex underlying mapping, we make them fit something easier to optimize: the **residual**. Specifically, if we want a few stacked layers to learn a mapping H(x), we instead let them learn a residual function F(x) = H(x) - x. The original mapping then becomes F(x) + x.

This simple mathematical reformulation has profound implications. Making the residual F(x) approach zero is much easier than fitting an identity map directly through a stack of nonlinear layers. The "+ x" operation in the formula is realized through **shortcut connections** or **identity mappings**. These connections add neither extra parameters nor computational complexity, but they provide an unobstructed "information highway" for the network. This fundamentally ensures that a **deeper network performs no worse than its shallower counterpart**.

Our poster clearly demonstrates the power of this framework through charts and data:

- On datasets like CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, Residual Networks (ResNets) completely solved the degradation problem. Even with depths exceeding 100 or 150 layers, the training error consistently decreases as depth increases.
- Notably, our 152-layer ResNet model won the **ILSVRC 2015** classification competition with an error rate of just **3.57%**. Its Top-5 validation error was as low as **4.49%**, significantly surpassing previous champion models like VGG and GoogLeNet.
- The results strongly prove that **depth is of central importance for visual recognition**, and residual learning is the key to unlocking this potential.

In summary, the ResNet proposed by this research not only set new records at the time but, more importantly, provided a simple, general, and powerful paradigm for training very deep networks. Its core concept of "shortcut connections" has become a **standard component** in nearly all modern deep neural network architectures, laying a solid foundation for the advancement of the entire deep learning field.

Our poster outlines the research context, core methodology, and remarkable achievements of this work. We welcome you to take a closer look and discuss with us further. Thank you!

在深度学习中，网络的深度对性能至关重要，但大家可能都听说过，网络越深，训练起来就越困难。传统的观点认为这是梯度消失或过拟合导致的，但这项研究揭示了一个更本质的难题：**网络退化问题**。即当网络深度增加到一定程度后，准确率不仅不再提升，反而会显著下降，而且这发生在训练集上，说明它根本是一个**优化难题**，而非模型容量或过拟合问题。

那么，如何破解这个深度困局呢？我们的海报核心展示了一种革命性的方案——**深度残差学习框架**。

它的核心思想非常巧妙：与其让堆叠的非线性层去直接拟合一个复杂的潜在映射，不如让它们去拟合另一个更易于优化的东西，即**残差**。具体来说，如果我们希望某几层网络学会的映射是 H(x)，那么我们转而让它们学习残差函数 F(x) = H(x) - x。这样一来，原本的映射就变成了 F(x) + x。

这个简单的数学重构带来了深远的影响。学习残差F(x)趋近于零，比让一堆非线性层直接拟合一个恒等映射要容易得多。而公式中的“+ x”操作，就是通过**快捷连接**来实现的。这种连接不增加任何参数或计算复杂度，却为网络提供了一条无损的信息“高速公路”，确保信号至少能畅通无阻地穿越网络，这从根本上保证了**更深的网络性能不会差于它的浅层版本**。

我们的海报通过图表和数据清晰地展示了这一框架的强大效力：

- 在CIFAR-10和ImageNet数据集上，残差网络（ResNet）都彻底解决了退化问题。即使深度超过100层、150层，训练误差依然可以随着深度增加而顺利下降。
- 特别地，152层的ResNet模型，在2015年ImageNet大规模视觉识别挑战赛（ILSVRC）中以**仅3.57%的错误率**夺冠，其Top-5验证错误率低至4.49%，显著超越了之前的冠军模型VGG和GoogleNet。
- 实验结果充分证明，**深度是视觉识别的关键**，而残差学习是解锁这种深度潜力的有效钥匙。

总之，这项研究提出的残差网络，不仅在当时刷新了多项竞赛纪录，更重要的是，它提供了一种简洁、通用且强大的深度网络训练范式。其核心的“快捷连接”思想，已成为当今几乎所有深度神经网络架构的**标准设计组件**，为整个深度学习领域的发展奠定了坚实的基础。
